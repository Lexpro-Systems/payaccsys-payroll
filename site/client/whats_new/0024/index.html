Added PAYE Over Deduction updates

Functionality has been implemented to enable users to calculate the Over-Deduction balance for each employee for each month, and subtract an amount (PAYE OD Debit) from that balance to decrease the employee's PAYE for that month. PAYE Over-Deduction items will be displayed on the employee's payslip.

PAYE Over-Deduction Credit panel.

The PAYE Over-Deduction Credit function can be accessed by clicking the 'PAYE Over-Deduction Credit' button in the drop-down menu section of the Edit Payrun panel:

PAYE Over-Deduction Credit button.

A PAYE OD Debit item can be added to a payslip by clicking the 'Add Item' button in the drop-down menu section of the employee's payslip:

PAYE OD Debit item.

If a user adds an 'Annual Payment' to an employee's payslip, a 'Use PAYE Over Deduction Credit' pop-up will appear, if the employee has Over-Deduction credit available. The pop-up will display the employee's Over-Deduction credit balance. If the user clicks 'Yes', the PAYE OD Debit item will be added to the employee's payslip.

'Use PAYE Over Deduction Credit' pop-up.

PAYE OD Debit item added through 'Annual Payment'.

Added Payslip Encryption

All payslips in a Payrun can be encrypted by selecting the 'Encrypt Payslips' option in the 'Payrun Options' section, after clicking the 'Process' button. Encrypted payslips can be opened by using the employee's ID number (or Passport number) as the password.

Encrypt Payslips.

Payslips can be encrypted individually by clicking the 'Add/Remove Encryption' button in the drop-down menu section of the employee's payslip:

Add/Remove Encryption.

Added UI-19 Form to Reports

A dismissed employee's UIF details can be used to automatically populate a UI-19 form:

UI-19 Form.

When clicking on the 'Reports' button and selecting 'Open' in the 'UI-19 Export' section, a list of all the dismissed employees of a company will be displayed. When clicking on the drop-down menu of a specific employee, the user can view, download or email the UI-19 Form of that employee.
