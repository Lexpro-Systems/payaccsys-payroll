Separated Earning and Resetting Rules

Leave earning and resetting rules have been separated, to make it easier to configure how leave is earned, reset, and carried over.

Resetting rules section.

When leave balances are set to reset, a default period must be specified in months. Leave balances will remain available until this period has passed. A separate carry-over period can also be specified to determine how many additional months selected leave balances should remain available after the default period has expired.

Default and carry over months textboxes.

Added Payment Period Date Accrual Option

Leave can now accrue on payment period dates, either at the beginning or at the end of the payment period.

Payment Period End Day option.

Added Workday Selection

Workdays should be selected for employees who do not earn a daily wage or have a defined work schedule. These workdays are used to calculate sick leave during the first six months of employment, where entitlement is based on days worked.

Workday selection.

Pro-rata Calculations

Pro-rata leave amounts are now calculated for the first and last month of an employee's employment, based on their employment start and end dates.

Payrun Overview Report

An additional option called "Detailed" has been added to the payrun overview. This will allow the user to view all of the payslip items of each employee in the selected payrun/period.

Detailed option in Payslip Items Report.

The report can also be exported to Excel, CSV, or PDF formats by clicking on the corresponding export buttons.
